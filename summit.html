The Gala / 祝賀会

01

An evening to honor the students and organizations lifting up Asian American and Pacific Islander communities at Temple.

The AAPI Awards Gala is Temple SASE’s summit: one night at the Temple Performing Arts Center to recognize the people and clubs who lead, build and give back.

Nominees are put forward by the Temple community. If someone has made your time here better, this is the place to say so.
