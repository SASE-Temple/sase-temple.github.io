What we do 關於我們

SASE is a national organization for Asian heritage students and professionals in science and engineering. Our Temple chapter has been around since 2017. Here’s what a year with us looks like.

Professional development

Résumé nights, industry panels with companies and networking events. Each year we also travel together to SASE’s regional (NERC) and national conferences (STEM Connect), which have some of the biggest career fairs and opportunities for students in STEM.

Cultural awareness

Every spring we head to Philadelphia’s Cherry Blossom Festival together. During the year we hold cultural celebrations and potlucks with other Asian student groups.

Community and service

Our main directive is just offering a space to bring the SASE community together. We host study sessions, game nights, and asian-culture themed social events to grow the community.

Common questions

Do I have to be Asian, or a STEM major?

No. Temple SASE is open to every student at Temple, from any major and any background. Our e-board alone covers health professions, math, biology, chemistry and engineering.

How do I join? Does it cost anything?

Join our Discord. It’s free, and there’s no application. Then come to a general body meeting or any event that sounds fun.

When and where do you meet?

We hold general body meetings during the semester on Main Campus. Times and rooms are posted on Discord and Instagram before each one.

What kinds of events do you run?

A mix of career and social. Recent ones include a potluck with Temple’s Asian Student Association, a professional bingo night with Drexel SASE, volunteering at the Philadelphia Cherry Blossom Festival, fundraisers, and trips to SASE conferences.

What are the SASE conferences?

SASE National holds a national conference each fall and regional conferences in the spring. They have workshops, speakers and large career fairs where students interview for internships and jobs. We share interest forms on Discord when it’s time to sign up.

How can I get more involved?

We open applications for e-board, freshman representative and general intern roles during the year. We announce them on Discord and Instagram, so keep an eye out there.
