About / 關於我們

01

SASE is dedicated to the advancement of Asian heritage scientists and engineers in education and employment, so that they can achieve their full career potential.

Our mission

Alongside professional development, SASE encourages members to give back to the communities they live in.

At Temple, that means résumé nights and industry panels, cultural celebrations, service in North Philadelphia, and a chapter of friends who show up for each other.

Open to every Temple student, from every major and every background.
